# Security policy

This policy covers my personal repositories on [@aeron-gh](https://github.com/aeron-gh).
If a repository has its own `SECURITY.md`, that one applies there. Projects in
[aeron playground](https://github.com/aeron-playground) follow
[their own policy](https://github.com/aeron-playground/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## Report a vulnerability

Report privately. Don't open a public issue, pull request or discussion.

- **Preferred:** open a private security advisory. Go to the repository's **Security** tab and
  click **Report a vulnerability**.
- **Or email:** [agab0323@gmail.com](mailto:agab0323@gmail.com), for example when a repository
  doesn't show that button.

Please include:

- what the problem is and what an attacker could do with it
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- the repository, and the commit or version you tested
- your name or handle, if you'd like credit

Never include real passwords, private keys, access tokens or other people's data in a report.

## What to expect

- I reply within 3 working days to confirm I got your report.
- I keep you updated while I work on a fix.
- We agree on a disclosure date together, and I credit you unless you prefer not.

There is no bug bounty.
